Who we are

K-beauty built on one idea: results you can feel.

VT COSMETICS is a Korean skincare and makeup brand known for turning clinical-grade actives into products people use every single day. We're not about a ten-step ritual — we're about a few things that work, used consistently.

04

Video beats

The shape of a video that converts.

Not a script — a skeleton. Keep it in your words, but hit these beats and make sure the product is on screen and in the caption early.

  1. Hook 0–3s
    Open on a real moment or a claim that stops the scroll — skin, texture, a before. No slow logo intros.
  2. The problem, briefly 3–8s
    One line on what you were dealing with. Relatable, specific, not a diagnosis.
  3. Show the product 8–20s
    Product in-hand and readable. Show texture and how you actually apply it — the demo is what people trust.
  4. Your honest take 20–35s
    What changed, over what time. Use "helps / looks / feels" language (see §07). Real beats hyperbolic.
  5. Call to action 35s–end
    Point to the product card, say the code out loud, and tell them exactly what to tap.
05

Tone & look

Warm, clean, and unmistakably you.

Voice

Like a friend

Talk to one person. Honest, a little excited, never salesy. If it sounds like an ad read, rewrite it.

Light

Natural & soft

Daylight or a clean soft key. We want to see real skin and real texture — avoid heavy filters that hide results.

Sound

Trend-aware

Use trending audio where it fits, but keep your spoken hook and the code audible. Captions on.

Product name legible, packaging clean and unobstructed, and please — no competitor products in frame.

06

The non-negotiables

Five things that must be in every post.

Miss one of these and the post may not count toward the deliverable. They protect you legally and make sure the collab is measurable on our side.

  • Tag the product card / add the product link

    On TikTok Shop, attach the VT product to the video so viewers can buy in-app. This is how the collab gets measured — video views and product-card sales are counted together.

    Most important — do not skip

  • Disclose the partnership clearly

    Turn on the paid-partnership label and say it — #ad or "paid partnership with VT." Visible, not buried in hashtag #37. This is FTC-required.

  • Say and show your discount code

    On screen and spoken aloud, e.g. YOURNAME15. Your VT contact gives you the exact code.

  • Tag the official handles

    Tag @vtcosmetics_official (confirm the exact handle for your region with your contact) in the post and caption.

  • Use the campaign hashtags

    #VTCosmetics #ReedleShot plus any campaign tag in your brief. Keep them readable, not spammed.

07

What you can say

Confident claims that stay compliant.

Skincare is cosmetic, not medical. Talk about how skin looks and feels — never about treating, curing, or healing a condition. When in doubt, use the left column.

Reedle ShotSpicule tech "Plant-based micro-spicules that help absorption." "At-home microneedling / injections / medical procedure."

Speak from your own experience ("for me," "in my routine"). Don't cite clinical or statistical claims unless your VT contact gives them to you in writing.

Keep it handy

The whole guide, in two columns.

Do

  • Use it in a routine you'd genuinely keep
  • Attach the product card and say the code out loud
  • Show real texture, real application, real skin
  • Disclose the partnership clearly and early
  • Speak from your own experience
  • Send the draft before you post

Don't

  • Make medical, curing, or "clinically proven" claims
  • Call Reedle Shot microneedling or injections

  • Show or name competitor products
  • Bury the disclosure in a wall of hashtags
