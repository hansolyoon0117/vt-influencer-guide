The story

A calm, honest end-of-day ritual — not an ad.

The whole video is your evening scalp routine. You come home, you wash with the PDRN Scalp Shampoo, you treat your scalp with the PDRN Hair Ampoule, and you finish with the PDRN Brush Hair Serum — then off to bed.

That's the hook, the middle, and the end already written for you. The PDRN line is VT's plant-derived take on the ingredient everyone's talking about, built into a full scalp-care ritual. Your job is to make it feel like the routine you'd actually do — cozy, unhurried, real.

Lean into the evening mood: dim warm light, a little "me-time," the payoff of climbing into bed with a treated scalp. That feeling is the sell.

02

The 3-step routine

Wash, treat, seal — in this exact order.

Show each step on screen with the product readable. Keep the order — it's also the order the products are linked above.

  1. Step 1 · Cleanse

    PDRN Reedle Shot Scalp Shampoo — wash

    Massage into a wet scalp and work up a lather, focusing on the roots. With CICA REEDLE® and plant-based PDRN, it leaves the scalp feeling clean, fresh and cared-for — the foundation for the steps that follow.

    "First I wash with the scalp shampoo — it's my reset after a long day. Feels so clean, kind of cooling."

  2. Step 2 · Treat

    PDRN Reedle Shot Hair Ampoule — on damp scalp

    Towel-dry, part the hair in sections and apply the ampoule directly onto the scalp, then massage in. This is the treatment step — VT's efficacy claim is hair loss, hair shedding & hair thinning improvement, with vegan PDRN + CICA REEDLE to nourish a tired-looking scalp.

    "Then I part my hair and drop the ampoule right onto my scalp — a little massage and it sinks in. This is my treatment step."

  3. Step 3 · Seal

    PDRN Reedle Shot Brush Hair Serum — brush through

    Finish by brushing the serum through your scalp and lengths with the built-in brush tip. Purifying & cooling, lightweight and non-greasy — the mess-free way to lock in the routine before bed.

    "Last step — I just brush this through. The brush tip means zero mess, and then I'm straight to bed. That's the whole routine."

Video beats

How to shape the 30-ish seconds.

Not a script — a skeleton. Keep it in your own words, but land these beats and get the products on screen and linked early.

  1. Hook — set the scene 0–3s
    "My everything-shower scalp routine before bed" or a real end-of-day moment. Stop the scroll.
  2. Step 1 · wash 3–11s
    Shampoo in-hand and readable, lathering at the scalp.
  3. Step 2 · treat 11–20s
    Part the hair, apply the ampoule to the scalp, quick massage.
  4. Step 3 · seal 20–30s
    Brush the serum through — show the built-in brush and the no-mess payoff.
  5. Call to action 30s–end
    "All three are linked" — point to the product cards, say your code out loud, tell them what to tap.
